01 / Executive summary

A practical digital operating system for the whole school

Schools do not need another isolated portal. They need one reliable operational backbone: a system that keeps every learner, staff member, payment, timetable, assessment and parent interaction connected to the same trusted record.

The proposal: deploy EduCore as the school's central management platform, migrate priority records, configure school policies and roles, train users, and launch in controlled phases with direct implementation support.
One recordStudents, guardians, staff and operations stay connected.
Every rolePurpose-built access for leaders, teachers, finance, parents and learners.
AnywhereResponsive web access plus an Android app for daily work.

The business problem

Fragmented administration

Spreadsheets, paper registers and chat groups create duplicate records, slow reporting and weak accountability.

Revenue leakage

Manual invoicing, reminders and reconciliation make it difficult to see balances, overdue commitments and collection performance.

Slow academic workflows

Timetables, attendance, scores, report cards and exam duties are repeatedly rebuilt instead of flowing through one process.

Limited family visibility

Parents and learners depend on delayed, informal updates instead of secure self-service access to relevant records.

04 / Launch plan

A controlled rollout measured in days, not months

Implementation is sequenced around the school's readiness. Critical records and high-value workflows go live first; advanced modules follow after core users are confident.

1. DiscoveryConfirm goals, roles, policies, data sources and launch priorities.
2. ConfigureSet school identity, sessions, classes, permissions, fees and payroll rules.
3. MigrateImport, validate and reconcile priority student, staff and finance records.
4. TrainRole-based practical sessions for administrators, teachers and finance users.
5. LaunchGo live with monitored adoption, issue resolution and post-launch review.

Recommended first-phase scope

  • School structure, users, roles and permissions
  • Active students, guardians, staff and class enrollment
  • Current-term fees, balances, payment channels and receipts
  • Timetable, attendance, assessment structure and communication
  • Payroll settings and authorized staff mobile access

Governance and safeguards

Tenant separation

Each school's operational records are scoped to its organization and protected by authentication and authorization controls.

Role-based access

Users see the modules and records required for their responsibilities, including linked-child access for parents.

Operational validation

Schools retain approval responsibility for fees, payroll, results, communications and statutory settings before relying on outputs.

Ongoing support

Implementation includes guided configuration, training and a structured route for support and platform notices.
