# EduCore Institutional User Guide

**Audience:** Platform Super Administrators, Proprietors, School Administrators, Academic Officers, Teachers, Finance Officers, Admissions Staff, Parents and Students.  
**Purpose:** operational guidance for using EduCore as an institutional school-management platform.

## 1. About EduCore

EduCore is a multi-tenant school-management platform. Each school works in its own protected workspace, while Platform Super Administrators manage the platform, school accounts, subscriptions and centrally managed academic resources.

The menu shown to a user depends on their role, permissions, activated modules and school configuration. A user should only see or act on data that their account is permitted to access.

### 1.1 Core operating principles

- Keep academic sessions, terms, classes, subjects and staff assignments current before entering operational data.
- Use official records and verify student, guardian, fee and score data before publishing reports or notices.
- Give staff only the permissions required for their duties.
- Use Draft, Published, Approved and Archived states carefully; the meaning of a status varies by module.
- Use exports and backups as controlled institutional records, not as a substitute for normal system use.

### 1.2 Main user roles

| Role | Typical responsibility |
|---|---|
| Platform Super Admin | Manages the EduCore platform, schools, subscriptions, platform settings, support and central Academic Content Repository. |
| Proprietor / School Administrator | Manages one school’s setup, staff access, academic operations, finance and institutional records. |
| Academic Administrator | Maintains academic sessions, classes, curriculum, subjects, assessment and report processes. |
| Teacher | Works with assigned classes and subjects; records attendance and scores; prepares lesson plans and notes where authorised. |
| Admissions Officer | Manages applications, admissions review, offers and applicant communication. |
| Finance Officer | Maintains fees, invoices, payments, expenses, procurement and payroll according to permissions. |
| Parent / Student / Staff portal user | Views only information made available through the relevant portal. |

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 Sign in and account security

1. Open the school’s EduCore address and select the appropriate login area.
2. Enter the account credentials issued by the school.
3. Complete two-factor verification when it is enabled.
4. Change temporary passwords immediately and keep credentials private.
5. Use **My Profile** to update permitted personal details and password settings.

Do not share a staff account. Create separate accounts and permissions for each staff member so activity remains traceable.

### 2.2 Recommended setup order for a new school

1. Complete School Settings and branding.
2. Create the current academic session and terms.
3. Create class levels, class arms and subjects.
4. Add staff, roles, permissions and teaching assignments.
5. Add students and guardians, or use approved import/migration tools.
6. Configure grading, assessments, fee categories and fee structures.
7. Configure portals, notices, payment gateways and notification channels as required.
8. Run a small controlled test: attendance, score entry, invoice, receipt and report-card preview.

## 3. Platform Super Administration

Platform Super Admin functions apply across schools and must not be delegated to ordinary school accounts.

### 3.1 Dashboard and school management

Use **Admin Panel** and **All Schools** to review platform activity and manage tenant schools. Typical actions include:

- create or edit school accounts;
- view school status, subscription and usage information;
- configure custom domains or white-label options where available;
- suspend, restore or otherwise manage access in line with institutional policy;
- enter a school view only when authorised for support or administration.

When impersonating a school, exit the school view as soon as support work is complete.

### 3.2 Subscription, billing and platform configuration

Use the Super Admin billing, plans, payments, broadcasts, support, white-label and settings areas to maintain platform-level commercial and operational settings. These settings affect multiple institutions; changes should be reviewed before saving.

### 3.3 Academic Content Repository

The **Academic Content Repository** is permanent platform infrastructure. It is not owned by an individual school.

Only Platform Super Admin can upload, review, activate, deactivate or remove repository resources.

1. Open **Academic Content Repository**.
2. Upload DOCX, text-based PDF, XLSX/XLS or ZIP resources.
3. Supply the best available metadata: authority, subject, curriculum level, topic, subtopics, version and usage rights.
4. Review extraction and import results. Image-only or protected PDFs may need OCR or replacement.
5. Activate resources only after reviewing their suitability, accuracy and rights status.

Activated, approved platform resources support lesson-plan generation. Teachers and school administrators cannot upload repository material.

## 4. School administration and academic setup

### 4.1 School Settings

Use **School Settings** to maintain institutional information, branding, contacts, academic preferences and enabled operational settings. Keep the school name, address, contact details, logo and report-header information accurate before generating documents.

### 4.2 Academic Session and terms

Use **Academic Session** to create sessions and terms, identify the current academic period, and maintain the academic calendar.

Before changing the current session or term:

- confirm score entry and report production for the previous period are complete;
- check fee structures and invoices are assigned to the correct period;
- communicate the change to staff;
- use rollover or repair tools only with appropriate institutional authority.

### 4.3 Classes, class arms and subjects

Use **Classes** to manage class levels and streams/arms. Use **Subjects** to create subjects, set their availability and relate them to appropriate classes. Ensure staff teaching assignments match the subject and class structures in use.

### 4.4 Curriculum and class coverage

Use **Curriculum** to maintain academic tracks and curriculum arrangements available to the school. Use **Class Coverage** to record or review arrangements for classes affected by teacher absence or reassignment.

## 5. Student lifecycle and student records

### 5.1 Adding students and guardians

Use **Students** to create, edit, search and view student records. Capture accurate identity, admission, contact, class, guardian and health-related information where appropriate.

Use bulk upload only with a checked template and retain the source file used for import. After bulk upload, sample records across classes to confirm data quality.

### 5.2 Student placement, transfers and status

EduCore supports class placement, student transfers, class transfers, archive/re-admission processes and status history. Use the correct workflow rather than editing historical fields directly.

- **Student Transfers:** record movement between schools or institutions where enabled.
- **Class Transfers:** move a student between classes/arms using the controlled transfer process.
- **Promotion Engine:** configure and apply promotion decisions at the end of a period.
- **Archive / Readmission / Reactivation:** use these for former, graduated, withdrawn or returning students according to the available institutional workflow.

### 5.3 Attendance, conduct, health and welfare

- **Student Attendance:** record daily attendance promptly and correct errors through authorised processes.
- **Discipline & Conduct:** maintain behavioural/disciplinary records objectively and with appropriate confidentiality.
- **Health Records:** record health information only for authorised welfare purposes.
- **Risk Flags:** use evidence-based indicators to identify students who may require attention; restrict access to authorised staff.
- **Skill Ratings:** record defined learner competencies in accordance with school policy.

### 5.4 Supporting services

Where enabled, manage hostels, transport, library services, visitor records, scholarships and alumni records from their respective modules. Maintain privacy and do not use operational notes for sensitive information beyond the module’s purpose.

## 6. Teaching, learning and assessment

### 6.1 Timetable

Use **Timetable** to configure periods, subject frequency, teacher schedules and class timetables. Review conflict views before publishing a timetable. When staff assignments change, update the timetable and inform affected staff.

### 6.2 Lesson Planner and AI-supported planning

The existing **Lesson Planner** supports teacher-controlled preparation of lesson plans and student notes.

1. Select teaching class, subject, topic and optional subtopic(s).
2. Record term and week as teaching context.
3. Set **Curriculum Origin Level** if the topic formally belongs to a different class.
4. Select delivery type: Regular, Carry Forward, Remedial, Revision or Enrichment.
5. Select **Generate with AI** to produce an editable plan using relevant approved platform repository material.
6. Review every objective, presentation step, evaluation item and reference before use.
7. Save as **Draft** while refining, or **Publish** when ready.
8. Use Print to create a PDF-ready copy through the browser print dialogue.

The target class always remains the class selected by the teacher. For example, an SS3 lesson can use appropriate SS1/SS2 prerequisite material without changing the lesson’s teaching class. Week selection does not prevent relevant repository content from being found.

Entry Behaviour is not part of the current institutional lesson-plan structure. Teachers can edit generated material directly; there is no approval queue between generation and publication.

### 6.3 Student notes

From a published lesson plan, generate student notes, review the content, edit it where required and save it as Draft or Published. The teacher remains responsible for factual accuracy, suitability, source use and classroom appropriateness.

### 6.4 Scores, examinations and report cards

- **Scores:** configure assessment types and enter/import scores for authorised classes and subjects.
- **Exam Timetables:** schedule examinations and check conflicts before releasing schedules.
- **CBT Exams:** manage question banks, examinations, sessions and controlled computer-based assessment processes where enabled.
- **Report Cards:** review calculated results, remarks, grading configuration and publication status before making results visible.
- **Transcripts:** generate official academic records only after verifying session, term, score and student details.

Never publish report cards or transcripts until exceptions, missing scores and grading anomalies have been reviewed.

## 7. Admissions and portals

### 7.1 Admissions

Use **Applications** to receive, review and manage prospective-student applications. Typical workflow:

1. configure the online admissions portal and its settings;
2. review submitted applications and supporting documents;
3. record screening/interview outcomes where used;
4. communicate decisions or offers using approved templates;
5. convert successful applicants into students according to institutional policy.

Do not create duplicate student records for applicants who already exist in the system.

### 7.2 Portal accounts

Use **Portal Accounts** to manage access for students, parents and staff where permitted. Portal users should only see records published or made available to their role.

## 8. Finance and procurement

### 8.1 Fee setup and billing

Use **Fee Setup** to configure school bank/subaccount details, fee categories and fee structures. Use **Generate Invoices** to issue invoices for the correct academic period and student grouping.

Use **Invoices & Payments** to monitor balances, record payments and reconcile payment records. Use **Payment Plans** for approved instalment arrangements and **Fee Reminders** for controlled communications with defaulters.

### 8.2 Online payments

Use **Online Payments** to configure available payment gateways. Test in an approved controlled manner before enabling a gateway for parents. Reconcile gateway notifications against recorded transactions and bank settlement procedures.

### 8.3 Expenses, procurement, inventory and payroll

- **Expenses:** record and categorise approved institutional expenditure.
- **Procurement:** manage purchase requests/orders and their approved lifecycle.
- **Asset & Inventory:** record school assets, allocation and inventory activity.
- **Payroll:** maintain payroll periods, staff salary settings, deductions, tax bands and payslips within authorised finance controls.

Segregate duties where possible: the person requesting, approving and paying should not normally be the same person.

## 9. Staff and human resources

### 9.1 Staff records and permissions

Use **Staff** to create and maintain staff profiles, job information and authorised access. Review roles and permissions when staff change responsibility, go on leave or leave employment.

### 9.2 Attendance, leave and workforce operations

- **Staff Attendance:** manage staff attendance, QR/offline controls and reports where enabled.
- **Staff Leave:** record leave requests and authorised outcomes.
- **Recruitment:** manage vacancies, applications, interviews and offers.
- **Staff Disciplinary Actions:** maintain controlled disciplinary records.
- **Class Coverage:** record replacement teaching arrangements.

## 10. Communication, reporting and compliance

### 10.1 Communications

Use **Announcements**, **Messages**, **Notifications**, **Auto Triggers** and **SMS Campaigns** for institutional communication. Before sending bulk communications:

1. confirm the recipient group;
2. verify wording, dates, links and sender identity;
3. avoid confidential student information in broad messages;
4. retain important communications in the relevant record where needed.

### 10.2 Analytics, exports and census

- **Analytics** and **Financial Report** provide management visibility; validate the selected dates, academic period and filters before relying on a figure.
- **Export Data** should be used by authorised personnel only. Store exported files securely and delete local copies according to policy.
- **School Census (ASC)** supports census-related reporting. Verify source records before submission.

### 10.3 Support and notices

Use **Support** to log or follow institutional support needs, and **Notices** for platform information. Include clear evidence in a support request: affected school, user role, screen, date/time, steps taken and any error message.

## 11. Recommended institutional controls

### Daily

- record student and staff attendance;
- record receipts and confirm payment exceptions;
- review admissions, support and important notifications;
- check timetable or class-coverage changes.

### Weekly

- review missing scores, attendance anomalies and risk flags;
- review lesson plans and teaching coverage internally;
- reconcile online payments and outstanding invoices;
- review outstanding transfer, leave and procurement actions.

### End of term

- confirm the correct session and term are active;
- complete scores, grading and teacher remarks;
- preview reports before publication;
- reconcile fees and financial records;
- archive required institutional documents and exports;
- prepare promotion, rollover and next-term setup using authorised processes.

## 12. Data protection and troubleshooting

### 12.1 Data protection

- Access student, family, health, finance and staff data only for legitimate school duties.
- Do not download or share personal data without authorisation.
- Remove access promptly when a staff member changes role or leaves.
- Use unique passwords and sign out from shared devices.
- Report suspected unauthorised access immediately.

### 12.2 Common checks before raising support

1. Refresh the page and confirm the correct school, session and term.
2. Confirm the account has the expected role and permission.
3. Check whether required prerequisite records exist, such as class, subject, term or staff assignment.
4. Record the exact error message and the steps that produced it.
5. Contact authorised school administration or Platform Support with that information.

## 13. Institutional handover checklist

Before declaring EduCore ready for normal school operations, verify:

- school profile and branding are accurate;
- current academic session and terms are configured;
- classes, subjects, staff and student records are complete;
- roles and permissions have been reviewed;
- assessment, grading, fee and payment settings are tested;
- portal access is configured deliberately;
- attendance, invoice, receipt, score and report-card test records have been checked;
- key staff know the daily, weekly and end-of-term procedures in this guide.
