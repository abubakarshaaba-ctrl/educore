## Short Description (80 chars max)

## Full Description

EduCore is the all-in-one school management platform built for Nigerian K-12 institutions. Manage academics, fees, attendance, payroll, and communication — all from your phone.

**For School Administrators:**
• Dashboard with real-time analytics and risk flags
• Student & staff lifecycle management
• Fee invoicing with Monnify, Paystack & Flutterwave
• Report card generation and publication
• Multi-school platform support

**For Teachers & Staff:**
• QR code clock-in attendance with geo-fencing
• Score entry with CBT objective auto-grading
• Lesson planner with AI-assisted notes
• Staff payroll with PAYE calculations
• Internal messaging system

**For Students:**
• View results, timetable & attendance
• Take CBT exams (objective & essay)
• Access fee statements
• Receive notifications

**For Parents:**
• Track child's attendance & results
• Pay school fees online
• Receive real-time notifications
• View timetable & messages

**Key Features:**
✓ Academic session & term management
✓ Timetable generation with conflict detection
✓ Automated broadsheet & report cards
✓ CBT exam engine with LAN mode
✓ Student & staff attendance (QR + geo-fencing)
✓ Online fee payment (Monnify, Paystack, Flutterwave)
✓ Staff payroll with salary templates
✓ SMS & push notifications
✓ Mobile app for all stakeholders

EduCore replaces multiple tools with one unified platform. Schools using EduCore report 60% less administrative overhead and 94% fee collection rates.
